## Scope

Applies to WorkBot support agents handling customer refund requests.

## Rules

1. A support agent may refund up to **$200** without manager approval.
2. Refunds above $200 require written approval from a support lead.
3. Do not promise refunds outside this policy; escalate if unsure.

## Notes

Record every refund in the ticket with amount and reason.
